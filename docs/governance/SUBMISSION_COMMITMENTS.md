## Frozen product proposition

IntelliLoop is intended to turn bounded, traceable software-delivery evidence into an Active Software Twin for one change. The planned end-to-end product:

1. brings requirements, decisions, repository observations, code relationships and validation evidence into one scoped Change Mission;
2. preserves source attribution, revision, digest, recorded time and explicit fact/inference labels;
3. deterministically reconciles comparable claims and exposes conflict, ambiguity, staleness and missing support;
4. traces explainable downstream impact and validation gaps;
5. provides cited explanations from a minimized evidence pack, with a complete deterministic AI-off path;
6. computes fail-closed release readiness from canonical evidence and rules; and
7. projects exactly one persisted assessment into a thin, immutable Release Passport.

This is the committed product direction. It is not a statement that every step was implemented at submission time.

## Non-negotiable trust and safety commitments

- Registered repositories are read-only inputs. IntelliLoop does not execute, install, patch, commit, push or deploy their contents.
- Candidate-source transfer remains prohibited; implementation is source-independent and clean-room.
- Controlled synthetic data is the only authorized competition demo data. Nisum, client, employer-confidential, MyTeams and unrelated private material are outside scope.
- External AI is off by default. The complete core and finale remain operable without an AI key or network call.
- AI may propose or explain; deterministic code owns comparison eligibility, canonical findings, impact, citations, readiness and Passport projection.
- AI cannot invent evidence, silently select a winner, waive blockers, approve release, mutate repositories or become readiness authority.
- Credentials are runtime-only if a later checkpoint is explicitly enabled; they are never committed, persisted or printed.
- A `FACT` label, passing validation claim, static code edge, inference, citation or green build is not automatically release truth.
- Missing, stale, invalid, unscoped, sample, fallback-only, AI-produced or unpersisted inputs cannot create `READY`.
- IntelliLoop is release-assurance decision support, not a deployment, compliance, safety or production-effectiveness guarantee.

## Submitted benefit commitment

The registration selected outcomes including time saving, reduced manual effort, improved quality/accuracy/productivity and better employee and customer outcomes. The submitted quantitative claim is a **pilot hypothesis** of more than 20 hours saved per month, illustrated as approximately 20–40 team-hours per month for a representative team of ten developers, one technical lead and one manager.

That range was not measured at submission and is not an achieved saving, guaranteed ROI or Nisum-wide extrapolation. It becomes externally defensible only after the controlled baseline-versus-assisted protocol in `IL-9.4` records:

- number and type of representative changes/releases;
- manual and IntelliLoop-assisted time for context gathering, impact analysis, evidence checking and release preparation;
- error, omission, citation, readiness and Passport-fidelity outcomes;
- feedback from developer, lead and manager roles;
- calculation method, sample size, exclusions and limitations; and
- a reproducible conversion from observed per-change deltas to a bounded monthly team-hour estimate.

No individual productivity ranking, hidden monitoring or unsupported production telemetry is permitted.

## Demo promise

The target demonstration remains one controlled synthetic retail cancellation change in which an apparently green state becomes `BLOCKED` for cited conflict and impact/validation gaps, becomes `READY` only after corrective evidence and explicit review, and becomes `STALE` after a bound dependency changes. The same normal product paths must create evidence, Twin, findings, explanation, assessment and Passport. A fallback must preserve truth and may not substitute hard-coded green state.

`IL-8.3` proves that sequence through the browser for the controlled fixture, and `IL-8.6` proves the recorded fallback uses the same release-candidate states with exact metadata and truth disclosures. Both remain synthetic local evidence, not proof of production effectiveness, release approval, signing or deployment authority.

## Claim-language rules

Use:

- `implemented and verified` only for behavior linked to executable evidence;
- `planned`, `target`, `pilot hypothesis` or `projected` for future behavior and benefits;
- `AI-powered` for the submitted product direction only when accompanied by the deterministic-authority and AI-off explanation; and
- `read-only inspection` rather than autonomous remediation.

Do not use:

- `production proven`, `guaranteed savings`, `zero risk`, `fully compliant`, `self-healing`, `autonomous release approval` or equivalent claims;
- the 20–40 hour range as a measured result before the `IL-9.4` paired human pilot is complete; or
- the submitted future-state workflow as a statement of current implementation.
